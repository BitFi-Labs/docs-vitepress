# Restricted Countries

The BitFi Public Sale is conducted through Sonar, Echo's public sale infrastructure for identity verification, eligibility checks, and compliant on-chain participation.

## Overview

Participation in the BitFi Public Sale is subject to identity verification, sanctions screening, wallet-level risk screening, jurisdictional restrictions, and applicable local laws.

Access may be limited or unavailable depending on the laws and regulations of a participant's country of residence or location.

## Restricted Countries and Regions

The Public Sale is not available to participants from restricted jurisdictions, including:

- North Korea
- Iran
- Cuba
- Syria
- Russia
- Belarus
- Myanmar
- Somalia
- Sudan
- Libya
- Yemen
- United Kingdom
- Mainland China
- Hong Kong SAR

Additional restrictions may apply based on sanctions screening, wallet risk screening, or the final sale terms.

## U.S. Participant Eligibility

United States participation, if available, is limited to verified accredited investors.

U.S. participants are required to use the locked participation track. Tokens issued to U.S. participants are subject to a mandatory 12-month lock-up from TGE and may be subject to resale restrictions under applicable U.S. securities laws.

## Verification Notes

All participants must complete the required identity and eligibility checks through Sonar. Sonar may also conduct sanctions screening and wallet-level risk screening.

Passing identity verification does not guarantee allocation. Final participation remains subject to jurisdictional eligibility, sale rules, commitment amount, and the final settlement outcome.
