# Legal Terms

The BitFi Public Sale is conducted through Sonar, Echo's public sale infrastructure for identity verification, eligibility checks, and compliant on-chain participation.

## Overview

This page summarizes key legal and risk considerations for the BitFi Public Sale. It is an informational summary only and does not replace the official sale terms, token sale disclosure, privacy policy, or any other binding legal document.

The BFI Foundation is the issuer of BFI and the seller in the Public Sale. BFI is the native token of the BitFi ecosystem and is designed for governance, staking-based utility, ecosystem incentives, liquidity pairing, and broader protocol participation.

## Eligibility

Participation is subject to eligibility checks, jurisdictional restrictions, sanctions screening, wallet-level risk screening, and applicable law. Access may be limited or unavailable depending on the participant's country of residence, location, accreditation status, or other sale-specific requirements.

Team members, investors, and other insiders are not eligible to participate in the Public Sale or in TGE airdrops.

## KYC and Sanctions Screening

All participants must complete the required KYC and eligibility checks through Sonar. The sale process may include identity verification, residence verification, accreditation checks where applicable, sanctions screening, and wallet-level risk screening.

Completing KYC does not guarantee eligibility, allocation, or the right to participate. Final participation depends on the sale rules and settlement outcome.

## Restricted Jurisdictions

The Public Sale is not available to participants from restricted jurisdictions. Current restricted jurisdictions include OFAC-sanctioned jurisdictions and other restricted regions listed on the [Restricted Countries](/public-sale/restricted-countries) page.

United States participation, if available, is limited to verified accredited investors and is subject to additional restrictions, including the locked participation track.

## Token Purchase Terms

The Public Sale uses a fixed-price, pro-rata settlement model.

| Track | Price | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Track A: Standard | $0.05 per BFI | 100% unlocked at TGE |
| Track B: Locked | $0.045 per BFI | 12-month lock-up from TGE |

If the sale is oversubscribed, each participant's commitment is scaled down proportionally so that total allocations match the available supply. Any unfilled commitment amount is refunded or made claimable according to the final sale rules.

Locked-track tokens cannot be transferred or sold during the lock-up period. Locked tokens cannot be staked and do not earn staking rewards while locked.

## Risk Disclosure

The purchase and holding of BFI involves significant risk, including the risk of total loss. Key risk categories include:

- Regulatory and compliance risks
- Market and liquidity risks
- CeDeFi, counterparty, and custodial risks
- Yield strategy performance risks
- bfUSD and bfBTC product risks
- Smart contract and cross-chain risks
- Governance and centralization risks
- Vesting, supply, and sale-specific risks
- Adoption, reputation, and obsolescence risks
- User-side wallet security and custody risks

No assurance is made regarding future value, performance, returns, rewards, exchange listings, secondary-market liquidity, or continued availability of any BitFi product or feature.

## User Responsibilities

Participants are responsible for:

- Confirming that participation is lawful in their jurisdiction
- Completing required identity and eligibility checks truthfully
- Securing their wallet, private keys, and recovery phrases
- Verifying official BitFi links, addresses, and announcements
- Understanding tax obligations and reporting requirements in their jurisdiction
- Reviewing the final sale terms before participating

Loss of wallet credentials may result in permanent loss of BFI with no recovery mechanism.

## Terms and Privacy

Participants should review the final Public Sale terms, token sale disclosure, privacy policy, and any Sonar or Echo terms that apply to onboarding, identity verification, eligibility checks, and sale participation.
